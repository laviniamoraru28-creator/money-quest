> A practical companion guide for discussing AI-generated financial suggestions with children.

## Explain the limits in plain language

An AI tool predicts useful-looking responses from patterns; it does not know a family's budget and can be wrong. Do not ask children to treat its answers as financial advice.

## Try a fictional shopping example

Show two hypothetical suggestions for a £15 budget. One ignores the price limit; the other lists assumptions. Ask which is easier to check and what information is missing. Verify calculations separately.

## Protect personal information

Use made-up names, fictional balances and imaginary purchases. Never enter a child's real address, school, contact details or payment information into a general-purpose AI tool. Adult supervision and age-appropriate access matter.

## Discuss fairness and persuasion

Ask whether a recommendation is sponsored, what evidence supports it and whether a cheaper or free alternative exists. Explain that popularity and confident wording are not proof.

## Finish with a human decision

Have the child state the budget, check the total, compare options and ask for help if uncertain. AI can be a practice example, but the educational goal is independent reasoning.

## Try this next

Choose one activity from this guide and repeat it with a different fictional example. Ask the child to explain a choice, show it with pictures or communicate it using their preferred method. Keep real family financial information private.
