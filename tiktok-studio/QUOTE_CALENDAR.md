## Quotations considered and rejected

- “An investment in knowledge pays the best interest.” (Benjamin Franklin): Not found in Franklin's papers or in any publication before 1818; reference works list it as unverified.
- “Becoming is better than being.” (Carol Dweck): Dweck has said the saying predates her; not presented as her quotation.
- “I got my start by giving myself a start.” (Madam C. J. Walker): Wording differs between sources and the 1917 newspaper source could not be checked.
- “Begin with the end in mind.” (Stephen R. Covey): Genuine, but the phrase is a registered FranklinCovey trademark; avoided for brand content.
- “The illiterate of the 21st century will not be those who cannot read and write, but those who cannot learn, unlearn, and relearn.” (Alvin Toffler): A later composite of two passages in Future Shock; the Gerjuoy sentence Toffler actually quotes is used instead.
