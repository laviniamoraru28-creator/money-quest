# Money Quest — Privacy Policy

## No accounts, no personal information

Money Quest does not require you or your child to create an account. There is no sign-up, no login, no username, no password, and no email address collected anywhere in the application. You do not provide your child's name, age, date of birth, address, school, phone number, or location — because the website never asks for any of it.

## The only choice the website remembers

When your child first opens the app, they choose a learning level (Early Learner, Primary, or Older Learner). This choice, along with a currency preference, is saved only in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, and it does not identify your child in any way — it is simply an interface preference, similar to a font-size setting.

## Learning progress

Your child's progress (lessons completed, badges, savings goals) is stored only in your browser's local storage. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. It is not backed up, does not sync between devices, and is permanently and immediately deleted if you clear your browser's site data or use the "Reset Progress" option in the app.

## What we do not do

- We do not use behavioural advertising or build advertising profiles of children.
- We do not use tracking pixels or third-party advertising trackers.
- We do not use third-party analytics that identify individual users.
- We do not request or use location data of any kind.
- We do not allow children to submit free-text personal information anywhere in the interface — every child-facing interaction is through buttons, choices, and structured activities.

## Cookies

The website does not use cookies for personalisation or behavioural tracking. If a technically necessary cookie is ever required for the site to function, it will be documented here explicitly, describing exactly what it does and why.

## Third-party services

See our current Third-Party Services list for the complete, up-to-date audit of any external service the website depends on, what it can and cannot see, and why it is used.

## Contact and information requests

If you have questions about this policy, or believe any information has been retained that shouldn't have been, contact us via the Contact page. For anything stored only on your device, use the "Reset Progress" option or clear your browser's site data directly — this does not require contacting us.

## Educational content, not financial advice

See our Terms of Use for the full statement — in short, all content on this website is general educational information, not personalised financial, investment, or tax advice.

## Changes to this policy

If the website's architecture changes in a way that affects what data is collected, this policy will be updated to accurately describe the new implementation before that change goes live.
