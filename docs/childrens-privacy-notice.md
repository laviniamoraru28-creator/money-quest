# Money Quest — Our Privacy Promise to You

## If you're 6-8

We don't need to know your real name. Just pick a fun nickname! We never ask where you live, your phone number, or a photo of you. Everything about how you play Money Quest stays on this one device — like a diary that never leaves your room.

## If you're 9-11

Money Quest only asks for a nickname (not your real name), your age group, and which country you're in (so we can show you the right kind of money). We never ask for your real name, address, phone number, or photos. Everything is saved only on the device you're using right now — not on the internet, not on a server anywhere else.

## If you're 12-14

Here's exactly what we collect about you: a nickname you choose, your age band, your country (for currency), and your learning progress. We never collect your real name, exact birthday, address, phone number, or photos. All of this is stored only in your own browser's local storage, on the device you're using — there is no account, no server, and no company database holding your information. If you want to clear it, you (or a parent/guardian) can use "Reset Progress" in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser — either one removes it completely and immediately.
