# Money Quest — Our Privacy Promise to You

## If you're 6-8

We don't need to know your real name. Just pick a fun nickname! We never ask where you live, your phone number, or a photo of you. Your grown-up is always in charge of your account.

## If you're 9-11

Money Quest only asks for a nickname (not your real name), your age group, and which country you're in (so we can show you the right kind of money). We never ask for your real name, address, phone number, or photos.

## If you're 12-14

Here's exactly what we collect about you: a nickname you choose, your age band, your country (for currency), and your learning progress. We never collect your real name, exact birthday, address, phone number, or photos. Your parent or guardian is the account holder and can see your progress. If you think something about your data is wrong, ask your parent to fix it from their dashboard — they can correct your nickname or age band any time.
