# Visual learning and discovery checkpoint, 29 October 2026

Implementation on feature/library-first-original-book only, in draft PR #4. No deployment, main merge, Godot, hosting or live-payment changes.

The original-book collection now has illustrated covers on responsive wooden shelves. Entire covers are keyboard-accessible links. A book opens from its cover into the existing two-page reader; closing retains the current spread during the visit and restores focus to the cover. Contents still opens any chapter without a question gate. Opening motion respects both reduced-motion preferences. Two additional story illustrations distinguish The Repair Table and The Kindness Club from existing covers. Published books remain attributed introductions and external links, not copied full books.

All ten Museum rooms have lightweight representative images and smaller hub thumbnails. Images are generated illustrations, not evidence, authentic artefacts or historical photographs. Every room carries a visible disclosure and a meaningful image description. The banknote teaching specimen is fictional and cannot authenticate real currency. Existing historical text, sources, answers and age challenges are preserved. Assets are self-hosted, included in the offline manifest and total 1,078,078 bytes for twenty Museum WebP files.

The new /en/explorer pilot contains three activities: What is money?, Choose and pay, Save for a goal. Examples precede two-picture choices. Optional narration, repeat, unrestricted next/back and keyboard access support children who are not reading independently. The examples are a pretend shop in GBP, not statements about real prices or universal historical payment practices. No scores, response storage, voice recordings, account or upload. Picture support is available at any age. This pilot does not mean that all existing Explorer lessons have been converted or that the design has been validated with children or SEND specialists.

Background sound lives in the persistent locale toolbar. It starts off and offers synthesized rain-like sound, wave-like sound, forest-like breeze and the existing original melody. These are not nature recordings. Sound survives internal client navigation, stops for narration, Quiet corner audio, tab hiding or page hiding, and requires a new action to restart. Sound selection changes stop the old context; pending starts cannot reactivate after stop. Nothing is stored. All new controls, pilot content and image alternatives have draft copy in nine languages; only English remains active pending full-site translation and review.

The homepage welcome is a still image with optional browser narration, visibly described as such. It is not a talking video and is not a human recording. Voice availability and quality depend on the browser/OS. The requested photorealistic speaking clip is pending video-provider connection and a reviewed cost quote; no credits, subscription, upload or generation were authorized or spent.

## Image generation provenance

All twelve new bitmap illustrations used the built-in image generator on 29 October 2026. Originals were inspected and retained; project files are resized WebP derivatives, with no semantic retouching. Museum prompts requested photorealism, soft museum daylight, no logos or writing, and illustrative rather than authenticated objects. Specific scenes:

- before-money: modern hands exchanging apples and bread, no universal-origin claim.
- first-coins: imagined irregular stamped ancient-style replicas on linen.
- strange-money: shells, beads and small stone disc as illustrative materials.
- money-through-time: pouch, ledger, unbranded card and phone side by side, no universal chronology.
- banknote-lab: invented paper specimen with transparent window, no denomination or real note design.
- gold-vault: plain gold-coloured bars beside a coin as comparison, no verified assay claim.
- around-the-world: globe, tokens and unbranded payment tools, no geopolitical map claims.
- business-invention: cardboard toy prototype, sketches and pencil.
- museum-of-mistakes: collapsed and revised model bridges, not a historical event.
- future-money: adult phone beside contactless terminal, blank screens, current technology.

Museum derivatives: public/images/money-quest/museum/{room}-v1.webp and {room}-thumb-v1.webp. Book cover derivatives: public/images/money-quest/books/repair-table-cover-v1.webp and kindness-club-cover-v1.webp. Book prompts requested illustrated storybook scenes with fictional Emma and Max, respectively examining a broken wooden toy car and sharing books, toys and seedlings. No titles were baked into images; cover titles are accessible HTML.
