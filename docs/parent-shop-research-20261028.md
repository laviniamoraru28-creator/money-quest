# Parent resources: evidence, pricing and launch requirements

Checked 28 October 2026. This is an implementation record, not proof that Money Quest improves outcomes.

## Learning and SEND design
- CAST (2024), UDL Guidelines 3.0: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/ . Offer pointing, drawing, speech, AAC and adult scribing; do not require handwriting or a spoken answer. This is design guidance, not certification or evidence of universal suitability.
- EEF, Universal approaches (2026): https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/inclusive-teaching/universal-approaches . Model a task, break it into steps, use temporary scaffolds and give specific feedback. This school-based guidance does not validate this home product.
- MoneyHelper, Talk Learn Do: https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/family-and-care/talk-money/talk-learn-do . Use everyday spending/saving conversations and supervised pretend decisions. Our activities are original; no MoneyHelper endorsement is implied.
- Use clear labels alongside task pictures, 16pt pupil instructions, generous answer areas and no timed tasks. Each worksheet has a reduced-distraction alternative with the same task. Do not label materials dyslexia/autism approved or claim to meet every SEND need. Adult support and choice of level matter more than chronological age alone.

## Parent-facing design and conversion hypotheses
Baymard checkout audit: https://baymard.com/learn/audit-checkout-flow-hidden-friction . Show the full price before checkout, clear digital format/licence and guest checkout. Use real sample pages and contents instead of testimonials. This research supports usability choices, not a promised conversion rate. Test sample downloads, product-detail visits and completed purchases using aggregate privacy-reviewed measurement only after consent/configuration; no tracking has been added here.

## Payments and consumer information
- Stripe hosted Checkout: https://docs.stripe.com/checkout/quickstart . Card details are entered at Stripe, not in our application.
- Verify payment server-side before serving a private archive; neither a success URL nor a client-provided amount is proof of payment.
- GOV.UK digital downloads: https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses/online-selling . Immediate supply requires express consent and acknowledgement regarding the cancellation right, with durable confirmation. Statutory remedies for faulty/not-as-described content remain.
- Before enabling purchases: verify merchant identity/address and adult contact details; tax treatment and territories; final terms/privacy/refund text; durable confirmation and download recovery; live Stripe keys and webhook endpoint; private persistent fulfilment storage; successful test-mode purchase, duplicate event, refund and retrieval checks. This is an operational checklist, not legal approval.
- Secrets never belong in source control or chat. Live payment activation and deployment have not occurred.
