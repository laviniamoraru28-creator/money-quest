# Money Quest World — Privacy

## For children

**You don't need an account.** You never type your real name, email,
address, phone number or a photo to play.

**Your game is saved on this device only.** Your character, coins and the
places you have visited are kept inside this web browser, on this phone,
tablet or computer. We don't get a copy.

**Please don't type real names or private things** when the game asks you to
name your business or describe an idea. Make something up!

**Websites outside the game:** some books and museum pieces have a "Read
more" or "Learn more" button. The game will first ask you to check with a
grown-up. It only opens the website if you choose to.

**Want to start again?** A grown-up can clear this website's data in the
browser settings. That deletes your saved game on this device.

## For parents and carers

**No account, no personal data collected by the game.** Money Quest World has
no sign-up, no login and no profile on our side. The game has no code that
sends gameplay, progress or anything a child types to us or to anyone else,
and it contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

**What is stored, and where.** To remember progress, the game stores a small
save in your browser's own storage for this website (IndexedDB) on this
device: the chosen character look, virtual coins and level, completed
lessons and missions, settings (such as Reduced Motion and read-aloud), and
any text your child types in the "build a business" activity. It stays on
this device. It is not shared between devices and is not sent to us.

- Clearing this website's data in your browser deletes the save.
- Private or incognito browsing does not keep a save.
- Some browsers (for example Safari) may remove website data that has not
  been used for a while.

**Read-aloud (off by default).** If you switch on "Read text aloud", the game
asks your browser to speak the on-screen text using the voices your browser
or device provides. With some browsers and voices, speech is produced by the
browser maker's online service, so the text being read may be sent to that
company under its own privacy terms. Leave read-aloud off if you prefer.

**Links to other websites.** A few book and museum cards link to official
sources (for example museums and publishers). The game asks the child to
check with a grown-up first, and only then opens the link in a new tab.
Those websites have their own privacy policies.

**Virtual money only.** All money in the game is virtual and has no
real-world value. Nothing can be bought with real money.

may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request)
for security and operation. **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: retention period and
