## Competency map, 20 activities per edition
Each numbered activity must identify the website lesson/experience it reinforces and one observable skill.

### Explorer 6–8 (highly visual, one action per page)
Financial literacy (1–8):
1. Cut-and-sort money / payment tools / not money, with example already completed.
2. Connect matching pictures of a banknote and its printed value; use locale-correct notes.
3. Match needs and wants using large picture cards.
4. Assemble a four-piece shopping basket puzzle and choose items within a simple picture budget.
5. Trace a path to a savings goal, passing save steps.
6. Order picture cards: earn, save, spend, share.
7. Find and circle safe ways to pay in a shop illustration.
8. Cut-and-stick a two-column collage: save for later / buy today.
Entrepreneurship (9–14):
9. Match a customer need to a useful product.
10. Assemble a market stall from cut-out pictures.
11. Create a shop sign collage with letters and images.
12. Match simple price labels to goods and customer budgets.
13. Put picture cards in order: idea, make, show, sell.
14. Choose a helpful change after a customer's picture feedback.
Leadership and social-emotional skills (15–20):
15. Connect a facial expression to a helpful response.
16. Complete a turn-taking picture sequence.
17. Sort helpful and unhelpful team actions.
18. Assemble a shared team puzzle, giving each character a role.
19. Select how to repair an accidentally fallen block tower.
20. Cut-and-stick a fair-sharing plan and choose a way to ask for help.

### Builder 9–11 (illustrations plus calculations and brief written choices)
Financial literacy (1–8): physical/digital money match; needs/wants budget collage; change calculation with local notes; compare price vs value; savings-goal puzzle; spot countdown marketing; digital payment safety decision; spending-plan board.
Entrepreneurship (9–14): customer-needs match; prototype collage; simple cost/price/profit grid; sign A/B test; feedback-to-improvement sequence; three-step ethical mini-business plan.
Leadership (15–20): assign team roles; sequence a repair after a mistake; emotion-and-response comic; fair budget allocation; listening dialogue match; cooperative decision board.

### Strategist 12–14 (realistic fictional cases and multi-step decisions)
Financial literacy (1–8): cash vs digital payment case; subscription cost comparison; opportunity-cost decision tree; monthly budget puzzle; price-per-unit comparison; savings timeline; advertising manipulation audit; safe purchase checklist.
Entrepreneurship (9–14): customer interview cards; value-proposition canvas; unit-cost and price calculation; profit vs revenue match; break-even puzzle with worked example; improve a product from feedback.
Leadership (15–20): decision matrix; stakeholder needs collage; constructive feedback comic; delegation plan; conflict resolution flow; inclusive team retrospective.

## Printable production rules
- A4, 25mm left binding margin, clear safe cut lines, large artwork, readable type, high contrast; colour and reduced-distraction versions.
- Every Explorer activity: one short instruction, picture icon (match, cut, glue, draw, circle, trace), worked example, large actual activity workspace and answer key. Never use tiny decorative pictures instead of usable task materials.
- Collage assets must be on printable cut-out sheets, with adequate space for scissors and glue; offer pointing, AAC, stickers or adult-assisted placement instead of mandatory cutting.
- Puzzles must have correctly matching pieces and a solution, not just a decorative image.
- Map to website content and age levels, but avoid copying an older learner's text verbatim into Explorer. No timed pressure or punitive wrong-answer language.
- Ensure notes, coins, symbols, budgets, maths and pictured denominations agree with the selected locale. Nine locales EN/RO/ES/FR/DE/IT/PT/NL/PL: GBP, RON, EUR, EUR, EUR, EUR, EUR, EUR, PLN. Locale-specific examples are not FX conversions. Store price remains GBP until separately configured.
- Each edition includes adult facilitation notes, full answer keys, non-therapeutic SEND adaptations, and clearly marked digital printable format. No claim that these activities have been clinically validated.
- Build free samples showing an actual completed Explorer page and cut-out sheet. QA with print test, legibility, page overflow, correct images, correct answer keys, all nine locales, ZIP inventory, download permissions and Stripe delivery.
- Do not set product ready or advertise finished files before all editions and translations exist.
