# Educational design decisions, 27 October 2026

These are design rationales, not evidence that Money Quest itself improves attainment or treats a condition. No efficacy study of this implementation has been conducted.

| Decision | Source | Application and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short explanations, relevant pictures and learner-paced steps | [EEF cognitive science evidence review, 2021](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/evidence-reviews/cognitive-science-approaches-in-the-classroom) | One focal idea per lab task, page controls in books, optional instructions. Illustrations are relevant to the adjacent topic. Attractive artwork alone is not a learning intervention. |
| Low-stakes recall with explanatory feedback | Same EEF review | Quizzes invite another attempt and explain a concept. A correct choice does not establish mastery. Practical challenges offer an opportunity to apply the idea elsewhere. |
| Avoid overstating cognitive-training transfer | [EEF research agenda and limitations](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects-and-evaluation/research-agenda-themes-priority-areas/research-agenda-theme-cognitive-science) | Brain Lab teaches strategies for specific everyday tasks. It does not promise greater intelligence, attention scores or general brain improvement. School evidence includes small and controlled studies; transfer to independent website use, ages 6–14 and SEND users remains untested. |
| Clear instructions before action | [W3C cognitive accessibility pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/patterns/o4p07-step-instructions/) | Explicit steps, persistent labels, labelled answer buttons and nonverbal participation. This guidance is not a substitute for usability testing with children who use AAC. |
| Make action outcomes understandable | [W3C feedback pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/patterns/o4p10-status-feedback/) | Visible feedback, status regions, saved-state labels and an honest storage-failure message. Browser and assistive-technology combinations still need manual testing. |
| Preserve financial accuracy | [Bank of England: What is money?](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/explainers/what-is-money) | Cash and account money are distinct from payment instruments. Debit, credit and phone payments have different funding implications. Coin Cove avoids implying that tapping creates money. |

## Differentiation

The three new labs contain 22 shared introductory questions and 66 distinct practical prompts. Explorer prompts use one choice or a concrete representation. Builder prompts introduce application and explanation. Strategist prompts ask for comparison, calculation, evidence or reflection. The introductory quiz is shared across levels; these are not 66 separate validated assessments. Learners can change level without an age gate.

Museum and Library add 54 practical prompts across 18 entries. Books remain short, three-page readings. Non-fiction entries include source links for grown-ups. Fictional activities are authored examples, not research findings.

Optional audio never starts automatically. Text remains available. Learners may point, draw, type or use their own communication tool. No microphone, chatbot, remote AI request or uploaded response is required. Mind Lab covers everyday wellbeing skills; it does not diagnose, score personality or provide therapy.

## Evaluation still needed

Observe children from all three age groups, including nonspeaking children and AAC users. Check whether they understand the instructions, find controls and transfer the idea to a new example. Include parents and educators. Do not infer educational effectiveness from completion counts, XP or a single multiple-choice answer.
