# Money Quest — Terms of Use

## What Money Quest is

An educational website teaching children financial literacy through lessons, quizzes, and games. All money shown in the app is fictional — nothing here is a real financial account, and no real money is ever held, transferred, or at risk.

## No account required

There is nothing to sign up for. Using the website means using it — there's no separate step of agreeing to create an account, because there isn't one.

## Educational content, not financial advice

Everything on this website is general educational information. It is not personalised financial advice, investment advice, tax advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell any financial product. Nothing on this website should be treated as a substitute for professional advice about a real financial decision.

## Your child's progress

Learning progress is saved only in the browser being used, and only for as long as that browser's storage isn't cleared. We are not responsible for progress lost when a device is reset, a browser's data is cleared, or a different browser or device is used.
